What the bot does

One symbol, one week, small trades with defined risk. You stay in control from the dashboard on the chart.

Stops on every trade
Each position carries its own stop loss, and you set the stop-loss and profit ranges.
Scores each signal
Trend, momentum and price-action checks are combined into a score. Optional filters check spread, volatility and the higher-timeframe trend.
Locks in profit
Quick-profit exits and trailing stops secure gains. Strong signals can run for a bigger target.
Daily and weekly limits
Targets and loss limits pause or stop trading automatically. Manual overrides are there when you need them.
Live dashboard
See balance, open trades, objectives, a seven-day table and your trade history without leaving the chart.
Trade log and analysis
Every closed trade is written to a CSV file, with win rate, profit factor, biggest win and loss and more.

Installation guide

About five minutes. Use a demo account for your first run.

  1. Install MetaTrader 5

    Install MetaTrader 5 from your broker and log in to a USD account. A demo account is the safest place to start.

  2. Download the bot file

    Save BTCUSD_Weekly_Challenge_v7.10.mq5 from the download section.

  3. Copy it into the Experts folder

    In MetaTrader choose File > Open Data Folder, open MQL5 > Experts and paste the file.

  4. Compile it

    Press F4 to open MetaEditor, open the file and press F7. The result should show 0 errors.

  5. Attach it to a BTCUSD chart

    Open a BTCUSD chart. In the Navigator, under Expert Advisors, drag BTCUSD_Weekly_Challenge onto the chart.

  6. Allow algo trading

    On the Common tab tick Allow Algo Trading, review the Inputs tab and press OK. Make sure the toolbar Algo Trading button is on. The dashboard appears on the chart.

  7. Allow the ranking address (optional)

    To join the ranking, open Tools > Options > Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add the ranking address shown in the bot's inputs.

Run one copy of the bot per symbol and magic number. Test in the Strategy Tester on real ticks before trading live, and keep the terminal and Algo Trading switched on while it runs.

Questions

Does the bot guarantee profit?

No. Trading BTCUSD carries a high risk of loss. Planned risk figures are estimates, and real results can differ because of spread, slippage and gaps.

Why does it only open one trade?

Netting accounts allow one position per symbol. Hedging accounts allow up to three at the same time.

The ranking says it cannot connect. What should I check?

Add the ranking address under Tools, Options, Expert Advisors, Allow WebRequest for listed URL. The bot shows the exact address to allow.

The dashboard text looks crowded.

Open Settings, then Display, and lower the font size with A- or press Default look. Auto-fit keeps the panel inside your chart.

Where is my trade log saved?

In the MQL5/Files folder of your terminal data folder, as a CSV file you can open in a spreadsheet.

Where do I get help?

Write to testing@btcbot.com and include your terminal version and a screenshot of the chart.
